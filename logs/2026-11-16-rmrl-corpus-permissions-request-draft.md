# RMRL request-scope audit and corpus-permissions draft

**Date:** 2026-11-16. **Status:** draft only; not sent.

## Audit of the existing request
`logs/2026-09-29-rmrl-request-draft.md` asks the RMRL reference desk for a PDF/scan of Iravatham Mahadevan's 1986 article, “Towards a grammar of the Indus texts.” It does not ask for access to the Mahadevan 1977 concordance or the Indus Research Centre corpus, nor does it ask for local retention, extraction, reproduction, attribution or reuse terms. It is therefore an article-access request, not a corpus-rights request.

## Proposed separate request (for an authorized human sender)

**Subject:** Request for corpus access and written terms — Mahadevan concordance / Indus Research Centre

To the Roja Muthiah Research Library reference desk,

I am evaluating the Mahadevan concordance and the RMRL Indus Research Centre digital corpus as a possible working source for a non-commercial research project on the Indus script.

Could you please confirm:

1. Which Mahadevan concordance edition and digital corpus are available, their format, coverage, and whether damaged/uncertain readings are preserved?
2. Who holds the relevant copyright or database rights, and whether RMRL is authorized to grant the permissions below?
3. Whether the project may access and download a local research copy for internal, non-commercial analysis.
4. Whether it may create normalized transcriptions and derived statistics for internal research.
5. Whether it may quote or reproduce limited excerpts, glyph records, or derived tables in a public research report, and what attribution/citation is required.
6. Whether any redistribution or reuse of the underlying transcription is prohibited or requires separate permission.

Please provide the applicable written license or terms, or identify the rights holder from whom permission must be obtained. We will not bulk-download, publish or redistribute corpus data unless the applicable permissions are clear.

Thank you.

## Research decision
Do not send this request autonomously. Do not acquire corpus data until the written response and any required user authorization are recorded. Access, local analysis, reproduction, attribution and reuse are separate permissions.
