## Falsification and uncertainty
The quick and full profiles are not run-for-run comparable. An unchanged method module does not establish full-pipeline equivalence. Task A remains unresolved; no claim that Indus is linguistic or non-linguistic is promoted. Any proposed language hypothesis must outperform adversarial non-linguistic controls and generalize to held-out inscriptions.
