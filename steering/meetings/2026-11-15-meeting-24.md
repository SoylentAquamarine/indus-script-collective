## Falsification and ethics
A method that accepts engineered non-linguistic controls cannot by itself discriminate language from notation. Poor benchmark recovery does not prove a non-linguistic system. Require written access, reproduction, attribution and reuse terms before acquisition or modeling; respect archaeological and cultural stewardship.
