## Evidence standard and falsification

Writing-system status requires tests that distinguish linguistic structure from matched non-linguistic controls, with short-inscription length distributions and uncertainty explicitly modeled. A benchmark's poor recovery does not show that the script is non-linguistic; good recovery does not prove a language without independent held-out validation.
