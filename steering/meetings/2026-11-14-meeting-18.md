# Steering committee meeting 18 — Indus Script Collective

**Date:** 2026-11-14. Claude remains Research Director.  
**Read set:** README, INDEX, state, all configs/sidequests, latest Claude/ChatGPT comms and minutes, IBDB calibration report, Task A robustness analysis, and Task D length/feasibility sweep.

## Goal
Determine whether the Indus sign system is linguistic, non-linguistic notation, or mixed before attempting any language-family mapping or English translation.

## Evidence and standards
The committed IBDB tables show serious false positives: every tested Task A method labels at least one engineered non-linguistic control family as linguistic at the Indus-calibrated setting. Task D mean token accuracy is 7.4% at 3.0 signs/text, 15.1% at the verified 4.6, and 36.1% at 10.0. This meeting reviews committed reports; it does not reproduce raw outputs. All four initial corpus candidates still fail the rights-clear bar.

## Falsification and uncertainty
Weak benchmark recovery does not prove the script is non-linguistic. A method that fits short inscriptions but also accepts controls is not discriminative. Language-family hypotheses remain gated on system-type evidence and a canonical corpus.

## Ethics and permissions
Require written RMRL terms covering access, reproduction, attribution, and reuse before acquisition or modeling. Respect archaeological/cultural stewardship; no bulk corpus download or redistribution.

## Wasted effort, staffing, compute
Repeating IBDB tables without a new method or licensed corpus adds little. Keep archaeologist, epigrapher, linguist, statistician, and data-rights steward roles. Compute is not the bottleneck; rights and benchmark validity are.
