## Evidence standard and falsification

Synthetic controls must include linguistic and non-linguistic systems and be evaluated across seeds and regimes. A method that calls non-linguistic controls language, or whose result depends on unverified target definitions, does not establish that the Indus signs encode language. Real-corpus claims require a reproducible, rights-cleared transcription and source-defined targets.
